About Us关于我们

A focused commodity trading house.专注、专业的大宗商品贸易商

Finavest Pte. Ltd. is a Singapore-based enterprise engaged in global commodity trading, established in March 2024. FINAVEST PTE. LTD. 是一家专业从事全球大宗商品贸易的新加坡企业，成立于2024年3月。

Headquartered in Singapore with an office in Shanghai, China, we have built a trading network covering Asia's major markets. We source and deliver physical commodities — principally copper and other non-ferrous metals — connecting producers and international trading houses with industrial consumers and distributors across the region. 公司总部设在新加坡，并在中国上海设立办事处，形成覆盖亚洲主要市场的贸易网络。我们以铜及其他有色金属为核心，从事实物大宗商品的采购与分销，连接全球生产商、国际贸易商与亚洲地区的工业用户和分销商。

Through our trade channels across Singapore, mainland China and Hong Kong, we have established a stable supply base and sales network. Alongside trading, we provide advisory services that help partners optimise trade processes and manage risk. 凭借在新加坡、中国内地及香港的贸易渠道，公司建立了稳定的货源体系和销售网络。同时，我们为合作伙伴提供贸易流程优化、风险管理等咨询服务，帮助客户提升运营效率。

Our Approach经营理念

Disciplined risk management at the core.以稳健的风险管理为核心

Every transaction is structured to protect margin and deliver certainty to our counterparties.每一笔交易都以锁定收益、保障履约为原则进行安排。

Back-to-Back Trading以销定购

Purchases are matched against confirmed sales, with product, price and quantity locked with both counterparties at the same time.采购与销售一一匹配，与上下游同步锁定品种、价格和数量，控制价格风险。

No Open Currency Exposure无汇率敞口

Purchases and sales are matched in the same currency, keeping each trade free from exchange-rate risk.采购与销售币种保持一致，规避汇率风险。

Counterparty Diligence严格的客户准入

We work with established producers, trading houses and buyers, supported by rigorous KYC and credit review.与成熟的生产商、贸易商及终端用户合作，执行严格的KYC与信用审查。

Reliable Execution可靠履约

Experienced operations ensure accurate documentation, timely delivery and smooth settlement on every trade.专业的运营团队保障单据准确、交付及时、结算顺畅。

Finavest's founding team brings more than 25 years of combined experience at Cargill, one of the world's largest commodity and agricultural groups. Finavest 创始团队在全球领先的大宗商品及农业集团嘉吉（Cargill）拥有合计超过25年的从业经验。

During their careers, the founders held senior leadership roles across Asia, overseeing regional operations in China and the wider Asia-Pacific, building long-term relationships with market participants, and managing risk across global commodity markets. 创始人曾在嘉吉担任亚洲区高级管理职务，负责中国及亚太地区的区域运营，与市场各方建立了长期稳定的合作关系，并在全球大宗商品市场积累了丰富的风险管理经验。

In 2024, they founded Finavest to bring this experience and discipline to a focused, agile trading platform serving customers across Asia. 2024年，创始团队成立 Finavest，将多年积累的行业经验与专业体系带入一个专注、灵活的贸易平台，服务亚洲客户。
